# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Added a 256x256 mod icon based on the Dimensional Configurator.
- Added multi-language support:
  English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, French,
  German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Polish,
  Ukrainian, and Dutch.
- Shift + right-clicking the air no longer clears the configuration while
  aiming at a DimChest or DimTank, even if the block is out of interaction range.
- Changed the project license to MIT and added GitHub build/release workflows.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Added the Dimensional Configurator.
  - Right-click a DimChest or DimTank to copy its configuration.
  - Shift + right-click a DimChest or DimTank to apply the copied configuration.
  - Shift + right-click the air to clear the copied configuration.
- Added the crafting recipe and creative tab integration.
